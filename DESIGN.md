# Design Research & System

## Fonts
- **Headings**: `Playfair Display` (serif) - Chosen for an elegant, premium restaurant aesthetic.
- **Body**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (sans-serif) - Chosen for exceptional legibility on cheap and high-end mobile screens.

## Color Palette (Ingredient-Inspired)
- `--brand-espresso`: `#3A2618` (Roasted coffee bean / dark wood)
- `--brand-paprika`: `#C0392B` (Paprika / accent action color)
- `--brand-olive`: `#556B2F` (Fresh basil and olive accents)
- `--brand-caramel`: `#D35400` (Caramel / warmth)
- `--bg-primary`: `#FAF7F2` (Cream / butter background)
- `--bg-surface`: `#FFFFFF` (Clean card surface)
